# Security

Baserate moves real BTC and MUSD. This page lists what can go wrong, what we built to stop it, and what we have not solved.

**Status:** no third-party audit yet. Three internal AI audit rounds are logged in [`docs/audit/FINDINGS.md`](audit/FINDINGS.md), and their fixes have been live on testnet since 26 Oct. Contracts are verified on testnet. Mainnet runs in proof mode, with hard caps of at most 2,000 MUSD of borrowing.

## Design principles

- **No new lending engine.**
  - Interest, liquidation and share accounting run on Mezo's own deployment of Morpho Blue, which has been audited.
  - Our contracts route into it and add limits; they do not reimplement it.
- **Immutable core.** None of the oracle, wsMUSD, the collateral receipt (`bcBTC`), the BorrowAccount implementation and its factory sits behind a proxy.
- **Exits never pause.**
  - Pausing stops new borrowing and credit draws; collateral-only deposits and all exits stay open.
  - The sentinel can never block repay, adding collateral, the full repay-and-withdraw exit, unwrap or liquidation. Top-ups are never limited by the position cap, which only stops new borrowing on a position above it.
  - Partial withdrawals stay open as long as the position remains within its LTV ceiling. With debt outstanding every withdrawal raises LTV, so a ceiling cut also limits how much can be withdrawn without repaying.
- **Decreases are instant; increases wait 48 hours.** Cap raises, ceiling raises, fee-recipient changes, module approvals and canary-operator grants all sit behind a public timelock. Cutting a parameter cancels every raise of it queued before the cut, and a queued raise lapses 14 days after it becomes executable. wsMUSD cap raises wait 72 hours.
- **No meta-transactions.** No ERC-2771, no `_msgSender()`, no `tx.origin`. The October 2026 veBTC exploit came from a forwarder bug.

## Threat model

| Threat | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| **Stale or manipulated BTC price** (the root cause of Velar's exploit) | Skip BTC/USD rejected if older than 60 s. Pyth BTC acts as a ±10% sanity band while its last print is at most 600 s old; the MUSD leg accepts Pyth MUSD/USD up to 3,900 s old. Prices are never taken from DEX pools |
| **Oracle outage freezes the market** | Morpho does not consult the oracle once a position has no debt, so `repayAndWithdraw` works with the oracle down. A divergence freeze from a lagging Pyth lasts at most 10 minutes, because the band stops applying once Pyth's last BTC print is 600 s old. Pyth updates are permissionless, though: during a crash steeper than 10% per 10 minutes, anyone pushing genuine but lagged prints can stretch it (round 3, R3F-C), and between Mezo's hourly pushes the band is off (R3E-3). The keeper has a `pyth.push` job that would close both by pushing the latest Pyth print every few minutes and before each liquidation, but it is off: Pyth's Hermes updates now need a paid plan, so for now the band relies on Mezo's own pushers. A Skip outage is unbounded, and is disclosed. Backstop deposits never read the oracle, its exits need none while it holds at most dust BTC (under 0.00001 BTC), and an exit's owner can always claim with the BTC valued at 0 |
| **MUSD depeg makes debt look smaller** | The MUSD leg is clamped to [1.00, 1.02]. Collateral is never valued above its USD price in MUSD terms. At today's ~$0.989 this undervalues collateral by about 1.1%, which is conservative |
| **Positions bigger than liquidation depth** | All collateral must enter through `bcBTC`, which only registered accounts can mint, so per-position and global caps bind the whole market, not just our UI. A position above its cap cannot borrow; extra collateral behind existing debt does not enlarge a liquidation, which Morpho sizes by the debt. Caps start at 0.01 BTC per position (testnet) and 2,000 MUSD global (mainnet) |
| **Inflation, donation and sandwich attacks on wsMUSD** | Internal accounting: nothing that lands in a block, whether a donation or sMUSD protocol yield, moves the price in that block. Unclaimed sMUSD yield counts nothing until a sync harvests it; harvested yield and every surplus then vest over 3 days, so a failing or short sMUSD claim never lowers the price or breaks a preview. Same-block sandwiches earn nothing. 1 MUSD of dead shares at deploy |
| **Manipulating vMUSD's allocator** (flash supply, a transient borrow, repay or withdrawal, a free force-deallocation) | The direction of a rate-driven move comes from 7-day trailing realized APYs (M1's supply-share price against wsMUSD's share price), which no transaction can move. No rate-driven move runs on M1 state changed earlier in the same block, and a checkpoint taken in such a block carries the previous outside liquidity forward, so a transient high or low is never recorded. Withdrawals from M1 are sized on the lowest outside M1 liquidity of the last day, so liquidity that appeared in M1 within the last day is never what vMUSD pulls against. Buffer top-ups never wait for the cooldown but run at most once per block; in a block where M1 was already touched they are sized on the larger of spot and the last checkpoint, so a borrow or withdrawal undone in the same transaction cannot trigger one. At most 1,000 MUSD per move, inside the curator's caps. The wsMUSD adapter rejects `forceDeallocate` while it is vMUSD's liquidity adapter (its penalty is 0 and every withdrawal reaches it anyway), and the next `rebalance` parks idle vault MUSD again, also during a cooldown |
| **Premium sniping, donation griefing and stuck exits in the liquidation layer** | Backstop deposits are priced at book plus unvested gains, so a late depositor pays for gains it did not fund and shares the next loss pro rata. Deposits are closed while the backstop holds BTC (0.00001 BTC or more), so nobody can buy in between a liquidation purchase, marked at 0.95 of the oracle price, and its sale above that mark. 1 MUSD of dead shares is deposited at deploy, and the deploy script stops without it. A matured exit is paid from whatever cash there is and the rest stays queued. The FlashLiquidator forwards only the BTC its own liquidation produced, and never reverts because a caller cannot take BTC |
| **sMUSD proxy upgrade or strategy change** | `redeemInKind` makes no calls into sMUSD's deposit, withdraw or claim functions. vMUSD's M1-backed share still exits without penalty through `BaserateAllocator.withdraw`, which retries M1-first when the wsMUSD leg reverts, and a 72-hour timelocked adapter rescue hands vMUSD's wsMUSD position in kind to the vault owner only if wsMUSD can no longer pay it out. When wsMUSD's withdraw reverts, Backstop exits are paid in kind (its idle MUSD plus wsMUSD shares worth the rest, which `redeemInKind` turns into sMUSD), and parking pauses for a day so sale proceeds stay idle and the BACKSTOP venue keeps buying with them; a purchase that would need the withdraw reverts, and the liquidator sells on a DEX instead. The FeeRouter never needs sMUSD to succeed: its reads are guarded low-level calls, a failed donation is kept in the router with its approval revoked, the treasury and bribe shares are always paid, and once sMUSD has kept failing across two weekly epoch flips its share goes to the treasury. An upgrade watcher that alerts and cuts caps is a planned keeper job |
| **Bribes nobody can claim** | The FeeRouter posts no bribe in the last hour of an epoch, when only whitelisted veNFTs can vote, and none on a bribe contract with zero votes. That share stays in the router and is added to the same gauge's next bribe |
| **Precompile and gas limits** (20 precompile calls per tx, 10M block gas) | Each flow's precompile budget is counted: open/borrow uses 2 Skip reads, exit uses 0. Deploy transactions stay under 8M gas |
| **Front-running clone creation** | Clones have no initializer; the owner is an immutable argument at a CREATE2 address salted by (owner, profile) |
| **Bad-debt seeding through canaries** | The canary profile (higher ceiling, for live liquidation demos) can only be opened by an allow-listed operator, at 0.002 BTC per position. Granting the role goes through the 48-hour timelock. A revoked operator cannot borrow, and can only withdraw down to the STANDARD ceiling |
| **Signed come-back claims replayed to someone else** | `comeBack` only claims for `msg.sender`, into the caller's own clone, and counts BTC by balance change |
| **Secrets in the front-end** (Mezo's July incident) | The web app has zero secrets: only `BASERATE_PUBLIC_*` variables. CI scans the built bundle for key-shaped strings and runs gitleaks on every push |

## Invariants we test

- wsMUSD's sMUSD plus MUSD backing is at least `totalAssets`, and its share price never decreases.
- A same-block donation, including sMUSD protocol yield, does not change wsMUSD's `totalAssets`, and neither does a reverting sMUSD claim.
- vMUSD's allocator never grows an allocation past its caps, never pulls M1 below the borrow buffer, and makes no rate-driven move when M1 was changed earlier in the same block. It runs at most one buffer top-up per block, even inside a loop of withdraw, rebalance and re-supply, and leaves no idle vault MUSD the wsMUSD adapter could take. Nobody can force-deallocate the wsMUSD adapter while it is the liquidity adapter.
- `bcBTC`'s native balance is at least its total supply. Every Morpho collateral holder is a registered account.
- Borrow accounts hold 0 MUSD and 0 `bcBTC` after every call. No entry point accepts the account itself as a receiver, and the owner can sweep tokens sent to it directly.
- LTV after any open, borrow or withdraw is at most the profile ceiling. Total M1 borrows are at most the global cap. No borrow succeeds on a position above its cap, and collateral top-ups always succeed.
- `repayAndWithdraw` succeeds while the oracle reverts.
- The backstop's share price only moves on realised PnL, never up instantly. A claim, partial or not, pays at most min(NAV at request, NAV at claim), and one paid in kind is worth at most what it reports. No deposit or mint goes through while the backstop holds BTC.
- Every MUSD that enters the FeeRouter is paid out or held as a recorded carry, the treasury gets exactly 20% of new fees, and no approval outlives a `distribute()`. This holds under failing sMUSD, dead gauges and the closing hour of an epoch.

## Known limitations

- **No third-party audit yet.** Three AI-assisted review rounds are logged in `docs/audit/`; round 3's Lows are open and scheduled. A third-party audit is planned before any cap increase.
- **Thin liquidation depth.** About 0.1 BTC clears cleanly on Mezo mainnet today, which is why mainnet caps are deliberately tiny.
- **Upgradeable dependencies.** sMUSD, MUSD's BorrowerOperationsSignatures and Pyth are upgradeable by their owners.
- **A broken sMUSD withdraw strands vMUSD's wsMUSD share.** Vault V2 only exits in MUSD, so if sMUSD's withdraw breaks, the part of vMUSD parked in wsMUSD cannot be withdrawn until it is fixed; the M1 part still can, through the allocator. The adapter rescue then sends the wsMUSD shares to the vault owner, who has to unwind them (`redeemInKind` gives sMUSD) and return the value. Until then the vault books that value as a loss, and value returned through the vault is rate-limited by its `maxRate`. After a rescue the sentinel should cut the wsMUSD adapter's cap to 0 so nobody deposits into the marked-down vault.
- **Backstop deposits close while it holds BTC.** Nobody can deposit between a liquidation purchase and the sale of that BTC, normally within the 6-hour auction; requests, claims, sales and purchases stay open. While sMUSD's withdraw is broken, Backstop exits arrive as wsMUSD shares rather than MUSD, and the BACKSTOP venue can only spend the MUSD the backstop holds idle.
- **Allocator trade-offs.** The trailing rule lags real rate changes by up to the 7-day window. Anyone touching M1 earlier in a block, even with a free `Morpho.accrueInterest`, skips that block's rate-driven move (R2V-4; liveness, not safety). Done before every keeper call, it vetoes rate-driven moves for as long as someone pays the gas, and it delays a buffer top-up until a checkpoint taken in a block nobody touched M1 in has recorded the low. We keep it: sizing moves on totals another transaction may have just changed would let that transaction steer vMUSD's funds. A borrower or an outside supplier can lower M1 liquidity across a block boundary to trigger a buffer top-up, at most one per block, which only adds M1 liquidity, within the caps.
- **wsMUSD harvest timing.** Unclaimed sMUSD yield counts nothing until it is harvested, then vests over 3 days, which is what makes a same-block or flash-funded deposit around a harvest earn nothing. The cost (R2V-5): a depositor who joins while a lump of yield is still vesting, even right after its harvest, and stays until it has vested gets a pro-rata share of it. More frequent harvests do not bound this; only the 3-day window does, and it ties real capital up for up to 3 days. The keeper harvests whenever 0.01 MUSD is pending, or at least every 6 hours.
- **The FeeRouter's sMUSD leg can be sandwiched** (R2X-5). `distribute()` is permissionless and sMUSD credits the donation to its holders at once, so MUSD deposited into sMUSD just before the call and withdrawn just after takes a share of that leg (40% of fees) equal to its share of sMUSD's supply. Flash-borrowed MUSD makes this free, but half the leg takes MUSD equal to sMUSD's whole supply (about 7.4 million on mainnet) for one transaction, and Mezo's Morpho, the natural flash lender, held none at our pinned mainnet block. It dilutes the yield sMUSD holders (wsMUSD among them) get from the leg; no principal is at risk. A fix needs vesting on sMUSD's side, which is Mezo's contract.
